# WD2 — The carrier holds exactly three cuts, and their readings close the boundary in whole turns

Sources read: FR1/IN1 (three cuts C₁ = K, C₂ = RK, C₃ = ιR; reading ρ = ½(1 + n·C)), EMK algebra
(R² = −1, K² = 1, KR = −RK), R38 (c_Σ = 1/√2; eight source events per block), RMG11 (two-valued transport),
WD1 (selection: boundary in whole turns), QT1.

Exact over the Gaussian rationals.

- **WD2-T1.** K, RK, ιR each square to 1 and anticommute in pairs.
- **WD2-T2 (no fourth).** An element of the carrier that anticommutes with all three is zero.
  Their product K·RK·ιR = ι. The carrier has room for three independent cuts and no more.
- **WD2-T3 (one eighth of the boundary).** For the readings along the three cuts,
  trace(ρ₁ρ₂ρ₃) = (1 + ι)/4: turn-part one eighth of a turn, size ½·(1/√2).
- **WD2-T4 (the whole boundary).** The eight such triples (signs ±, outward sense) each carry one
  eighth of a turn; together exactly one full turn. Half the boundary gives −1.
- **WD2-T5.** With two cuts only, every such product is real: no turn is carried.

What this changes in WD1: there, "three directions" was selected by asking for whole turns. Here the
order is reversed and nothing is asked: the carrier admits three cuts (T2), and for three cuts the
boundary closes in one full turn of the two-valued reading (T4) — the two turns of WD1-T1. The eight
pieces and the size 1/√2 are R38's eight events and c_Σ, met from the side of directions.

Put in: directions of space are identified with cuts of the carrier (FR1). Not claimed: that this
identification is forced; that T4's count of eight is the same fact as R38's (same numbers, not proved same).
Open gate: QT1's unit — with the boundary's full turn made of eight equal pieces, test "one unit per piece"
against the count: area/4 over eight pieces.
