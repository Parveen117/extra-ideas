# FC1 — what a first cut that is its own dagger leaves out

Stage of the uncut line. No measurement. Exact (sympy): `fc1_what_the_first_cut_leaves_out.py`, `test_fc1.py` (4 tests).

The owner's question: today's physics and mathematics stand on a first cut made at λ = 0. Was something
missed at that cut — a sine, a vertical component, a dimension — by the frame or by flatness? If so physics
could give something predictable. E = mc² also assumes flatness, so it is not the complete equation either.

UP7-T1: every cut is κ = uK + vS + wι with u² + v² − w² = 1. A cut that is its own dagger has w = 0. That one
coordinate — the part of the cut along the turn — is what the flat first cut does not have. It is not a new

## Statements (the mass relation)

A reading is ρ = a + bK + cS + dι; a change of frame is ρ → GρG†, det G = 1.

**T1 (two invariants, not one).** Under every change of frame

  m² = a² − b² − c²  and  W = d

are kept separately, and det ρ = m² + W². The flat relation E² − p² = m² is exact and stays exact.
It is the relation of the self-dagger part of the reading; the turn-part W is a second invariant beside it.

**T2 (the sine).** At rest an element of determinant M² is M·Exp(θι): the energy read is M cos θ, the
turn-part is M sin θ, and

  M² = E₀² + W².

A boost mixes the energy with the momentum and never touches W.

**T3 (who sees W).** No quadratic reading contains W: X·ρX is the same with or without it. The return around
a loop is 2W × Area.

**T4 (W adds).** For two readings, det(ρ₁ + ρ₂) = m₁₂² + (W₁ + W₂)². Turn-parts add; opposite ones cancel and
the pair is flat.

**T5 (witness).** M = 5, W = 3. At rest E = 4. Moving at 3/5: E = 5, p = 3, W = 3, E² − p² = 16, det = 25.

## Answer

Yes: one thing is left out, and it is the sine. E = mc² is complete for what a self-dagger cut can read —
with m the invariant of the self-dagger part it has no correction. It is not complete as a statement about
the whole element: the whole has determinant M² = (mc²)² + W², and the part W = M sin θ is carried in the
turn, is the same in every frame, adds from one system to the next, and is invisible to every energy
reading. Only a cyclic reading returns it.

The form of a prediction follows, without a number: for a system with W ≠ 0, the mass obtained from energy
readings (m) and the size obtained from how elements compose (M) differ by the factor cos θ, and the
difference is exactly what a loop reads.

## What is put in, what is not claimed

* The identification of the scalar part with energy and of (b, c) with momentum is the reading of IN1 and GB1
  on this carrier, where it gives two space directions. Three space directions (the block of OB1) are not
  treated; there the turn commutes with the cuts and this W has to be found again.
* No physical system is shown to have W ≠ 0. No number is predicted.
* Whether a dimension was absent at the first cut is not answered: this stage finds a missed component inside
  the carrier, not a missed dimension.
