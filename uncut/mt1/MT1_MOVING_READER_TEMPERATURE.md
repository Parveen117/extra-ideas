# MT1 — The temperature of a body read by a moving reader: three answers, three readings of one function

Sources read: HX1 (boost = turn by an imaginary angle), UP6 (scale operations E[x|y]: a derivative is
defined by what is held), PL1 (count = areas in planes, one Maxwell-type equation per plane), ST1-T4
(T = 1/period), CI1 (exp(I₀) = cosh ψ; exp(2I_cut) = C_P/C_V), XU2 (two readings, opposite results).

Body at rest: centre value E₀(S), T₀ = dE₀/dS. Reader at rapidity φ: (E′, p′) = E₀·(cosh φ, sinh φ), γ = cosh φ.

- **MT1-T1 (count against velocity).** ∂E′/∂S at fixed velocity = γ·T₀.
- **MT1-T2 (count against momentum).** ∂E′/∂S at fixed momentum = T₀/γ.
- **MT1-T3 (rest reading).** T₀ is the geometric mean of the two.
  The three values long proposed for a moving body — γT₀, T₀/γ, T₀ — are these three readings of one function.
- **MT1-T4 (the line's rule picks one).** The closed turn's period in the moving reader's time is γ·P₀, so
  T = 1/period = T₀/γ: the fixed-momentum reading.
- **MT1-T5 (a fourth plane).** dE′ = (T₀/γ)·dS + v·dp′, closed: momentum is one more plane, with the velocity
  as its partner exactly as Φ and Ω are for charge and spin (PL1).
- **MT1-T6.** (reading 1)/(reading 2) = cosh²φ = exp(2I₀): the ratio of the two contested answers is the
  shared information between the two frames — the same form as C_P/C_V = exp(2I_cut) (CI1).
- **MT1-T7.** The count is reader-free only with the momentum plane included: P′(dE′ − v·dp′) = dS, while
  P′·dE′ alone is γ²·dS.

Reading: the disagreement is a choice of corner of one diagram (E′, S; v, p′), not a disagreement about
nature. The rule "T is the inverse period of the closed turn in the reader's own time" selects T₀/γ, and the
count stays invariant only if the momentum plane is kept.

Put in: (E′, p′) = E₀(cosh φ, sinh φ); dilation of the period; T = 1/period. That the classical proposals
differ by what is held fixed has been said before in other words (general knowledge, not checked against
the literature here); the stage's own content is T4, T5, T6 and T7.
Not claimed: an experiment that separates the readings; anything about heat flow between bodies in relative motion.
