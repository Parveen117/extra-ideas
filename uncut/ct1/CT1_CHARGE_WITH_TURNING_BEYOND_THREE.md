# CT1 — Charge with turning beyond three directions: a consistent candidate refused, and the law that stands

Sources read: TS2 (first law + pure-power forms fixed the share), LC2 (stored cost in any d), HD1, HL1 (charge with
turning in d ≥ 4 not among its cases), UN2/RT1 (rim reader's unit N = cos χ).
External, read at the source: A. N. Aliev, "Rotating black holes in higher dimensional Einstein-Maxwell gravity"
(arXiv:hep-th/0604207), eq. (17) (weakly charged solution) and eq. (22) (its horizon function). Its N is our d.

## A candidate by TS2's method

Forms: TS2 with ρ = 1 for the turning; LC2's stored cost added at the inner radius; charge Q = q·γ^ν.

- **CT1-T1.** With ν = (d−3)/(d−1) the candidate satisfies the first law identically, with Ω = v/R, for d = 3…6.
  In three directions it is the known charged turning centre exactly.
- **CT1-T2.** Its energy at second order in the charge, at fixed count and spin, is the static stored cost times
  [(d−3) + 2cos²χ]/(d−1) · cos^{2(d−3)/(d−1)}χ.

## The check

- **CT1-T3.** From eq. (17) the co-turning potential is the same all over the horizon and equals
  Q·cos²χ / ((d−2)·r₊^{d−2}): the static value times N_rim².
- **CT1-T4 (refusal).** The candidate's factor equals cos²χ only for d = 3. At χ = 1: 0.350, 0.349, 0.342 for
  d = 4, 5, 6 against 0.292. A first-law-consistent, pure-power candidate is not the weakly charged solution.

This also bounds TS2: there the same method landed on the published family; here it does not. First-law
consistency with pure-power forms is a filter, not a derivation.

## What stands

- **CT1-T5.** The weakly charged horizon function (eq. 22) is HL1's form with the stored cost added to the
  turning factor: P = (1 + a²/r²) + q²·r^{−2(d−2)}.
- **CT1-T6 (second-order law, one variable).** With u = S/r₊ (count over inner radius) and K = (d−1)/(4π):

    M = K·[ u + (s·q)²/u ]

  - exact for the neutral turning centre in every d (HD1);
  - exact for the static charged centre in every d (LC2);
  - for charge and turning together, the second term is the energy at second order in the charge at fixed
    count and spin: the static stored cost times N_rim², as eq. (17) requires.
  The centre value and the stored cost are the two reciprocal functions of one variable.

Put in: energy at second order = ½·(potential)·(charge) at fixed count and spin; the source's weakly charged
solution. Not claimed: the form of T6 beyond second order in the charge when both planes are present (in three
directions the exact centre is not of that form in its own inner radius); a closed solution in d ≥ 4.
Open gate: the next order — whether the first law, with T5's P and HL1's heat reading, fixes the fourth-order term.
