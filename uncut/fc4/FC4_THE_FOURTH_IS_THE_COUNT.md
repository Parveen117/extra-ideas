# FC4 — The fourth component of a reading is its count, not a fourth direction

Owner's remark for this stage: the fourth dimension can be merged with the three — changes in the three are
measured in the observer's own fourth; no extra dimension, no external clock, is needed for a measurement.

Sources read: FR1 (three anticommuting cuts K, RK, ιR; no fourth — PROVED), IN1 (reading tensor ρ = ½(n + r·C);
det ρ = ¼(n² − r·r), the same in every frame — PROVED), WD2 (the product of the three cuts is ι), HX1, CL1 (time is
a count of events along a history; no external unit of time in the native calculus), MT1, ST1, HD1/CT1.

Native dagger: conjugate the coefficients, keep K and S, reverse R.

- **FC4-T1.** The self-dagger elements of the three-cut carrier have exactly four real components: ρ = n + r·C.
- **FC4-T2.** det ρ = n² − r·r (IN1-T1): one component against three, with opposite sign.
- **FC4-T3 (different in kind).** The n-part commutes with every cut; the three cuts anticommute among
  themselves; and nothing anticommutes with all three. The fourth component is not a fourth direction.
- **FC4-T4.** When readings are put together their n-parts add: n is a count.
- **FC4-T5.** A reading at rest has only its count. For a reader in motion the count becomes n·cosh ψ and a
  direction part n·sinh ψ appears; det is unchanged.
- **FC4-T6.** The turn that closes a reading is ι = C₁C₂C₃, the product of the three cuts.
- **FC4-T7.** For a reading at rest the invariant is n²: a reader's own time is its own count.

So the remark is exact in the carrier: "four dimensions" is the size of the space of readings of three cuts —
three directions and the reader's own count — and the clock that closes a reading is made of the three cuts
themselves. Nothing has to be added from outside.

Consequence for the recent stages: a fourth, fifth, … space direction would need a fourth cut, which this
carrier does not have. The cases d ≥ 4 in HD1, TS2 and CT1 are therefore tests of the rules against published
families, not statements about this carrier; in the carrier d = 3, where TS2 gives the plain boost with
share 1 and no rim rule is needed.

Put in: nothing beyond FR1, IN1 and the native dagger; T1, T3 and T6 restate proved results in one place,
T4, T5, T7 are their direct consequences. Not claimed: why nature's carrier is this one (WD1/WD2's open point).
