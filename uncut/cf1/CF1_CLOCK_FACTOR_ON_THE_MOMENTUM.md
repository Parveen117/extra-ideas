# CF1 — Why the rim's momentum carries the clock factor; the published formula checked at its source

Sources read: ET2 (the factor 1 − h²R² found by matching; open gate), MT1 (local quadrature: energy = √(rest² +
momentum²)), AR1-T4 and UN2 (a reading of energy brought to another reader scales with the clock factor), SP1,
LP1, XU2, CO1.
External: Caldarelli, Cognola, Klemm, "Thermodynamics of Kerr-Newman-AdS black holes and conformal field
theories" (arXiv:hep-th/9908022), eq. (43), read from the source for this stage.

- **CF1-T1 (the rule).** At the rim the reading is local: E_loc = √(m_loc² + p²), p = J/R. Brought to the far
  reader by the clock factor N of the place: E = N·E_loc. With M₀ = N·m_loc the far-read rest value,
  E² = M₀² + N²·p².
  SP1 is the case N = 1.
- **CF1-T2 (against the source).** With R = √(S/π), M₀ = R/2 + Q²/(2R) + R³/(2l²) (stored-cost rule) and
  N² = 1 + R²/l², the rule gives eq. (43) exactly, term by term.
- **CF1-T3 (which clock factor).** N² is the clock factor of the surrounding space with the centre removed
  (M = 0, Q = 0); the centre's own terms are already inside M₀, which is the value of M that puts the full
  clock factor to zero at R.
- **CF1-T4.** The expanding space of ET2 is the same statement with 1/l² → −h².
- **CF1-T5.** The partner of spin becomes Ω = N·v/R, v the rim speed as read far.

Standing of the joining rules after this stage:
1. a plane whose partner is spread at least cost adds its stored cost to the rest value (charge, LP1; the
   space's own term, XU2);
2. a plane carried by the boundary as motion adds in quadrature, locally, and is brought to the reader by the
   clock factor of the surrounding space (spin; MT1 + UN2).
ET2-T2c is no longer a fit: it is rule 2 with the space's clock factor.

Put in: in T3, that the factor is the surrounding space's and not the full one (the full one vanishes at R);
the continuation of eq. (43) to an expanding space (T4) is this stage's step, not a statement of the source.
Not claimed: a new mass formula — eq. (43) is the source's; the content here is that two rules of the line
produce it. Open gate: an independent case where rule 2 can fail — a centre in more than three directions,
where the published formulas have more than one spin.

> Later note (HD1): rule 2 in the form E² = M₀² + N²p² is special to three directions. In d directions the form that holds is rim radius R = r₊/N and M = M₀·γ^{2/(d−1)} at fixed count; see `uncut/hd1` and the single form in `uncut/hl1`.
