# XU1 — In an expanding space no frame has the full unit; the count read by the best frame depends on the path

Sources read: UN2 (unit of a frame = its clock factor N; far frame has unit 1), SM1 (only the unit can vary),
PL2 (dθ = dk ∧ dS), CO1 (expanding space, fall speed hX), MO1, BL1 (open gate: what selects the sense).

Clock factor N² = 1 − 2M/r − h²r² (centre M in a space expanding at rate h).

- **XU1-T1 (the best frame).** N is largest at r* = (M/h²)^{1/3}, where N² = 1 − 3(hM)^{2/3}.
  No frame has unit 1; the best available unit is c_f = √(1 − 3(hM)^{2/3}).
- **XU1-T2.** h → 0 gives back unit 1 (UN2). At 27h²M² = 1 the unit is 0: no frame can read a count.
- **XU1-T4 (the defect).** Count read with that unit: θ = dS/c_f. On the plane (size r_b, rate h):
  dθ = −(∂(1/c_f)/∂h)·2πr_b. It is not zero: the count is not a function of state.
- **XU1-T5 (one sign).** ∂(1/c_f)/∂h > 0 wherever a best frame exists (h·r_b < 1/√3). The defect has the same
  sign over the whole region.
- **XU1-T6 (one circuit).** Grow r_b from 1 to 2 at h = 1/10, raise h to 1/5, shrink back, lower h:
  the count changes by −8.7051346. The opposite circuit — grow while the expansion is fast, shrink while it
  is slow — raises it by the same amount, every time.

What this gives: here nothing was chosen. The unit comes from UN2, the angle is fixed by SM1, and the
expansion removes the frame that had the full unit. The sense in which the count rises is then fixed by the
sign of one derivative, the same everywhere.

Put in: (i) the reading frame is the one with the largest clock factor; (ii) the expansion rate h is treated
as a variable of state; (iii) M is kept as the centre value while the unit changes — UN2-T5's caveat stands:
a full local statement needs the energy read by the same frame, and that is not done here.
Not claimed: that this is the arrow of time; a physical process that runs the circuit.
Open gate: (iii). Read M with the same frame's unit and recompute dθ; if the defect survives with one sign,
the result is independent of the caveat.
