# XU2 — The expansion has its own plane (its partner is a volume); XU1's sense, tested with the frame's own energy

Sources read: XU1 (open gate: read M with the same frame's unit), PL1 (count = areas in planes, one
Maxwell-type equation per plane), ST1, UN2-T5, CO1.

Variables: size r_b of the centre's horizon, expansion rate h. M = r_b(1 − h²r_b²)/2.

**(i) A third plane.**
- **XU2-T1.** With h varying, the (M, t)-area alone is not closed:
  curl = 8πh·r_b³(3h²r_b² − 2)/(3h²r_b² − 1)².
- **XU2-T2.** dS = P·dM + P·(h·r_b³)·dh exactly. The expansion is a third plane beside (M, t) and
  (charge or spin, angle); the partner of h² is r_b³/2 — a volume. With it the flat count is again a
  function of state.

**(ii) The sense of XU1, tested.**
- **XU2-T3.** Two readings by the best frame (unit c = √(1 − 3(hM)^{2/3})), at seven sample states:
  - A: count form with the frame's unit only (XU1): defect negative at all seven;
  - F: energy also read by the frame (M/c): defect positive at all seven.
  Neither is ever closed. The path-dependence survives the caveat of XU1; **the sense does not — it
  reverses.** XU1-T6's statement about which circuit raises the count holds for reading A only.

**(iii) Both horizons.**
- **XU2-T4.** The outer horizon r_c = (−r_b + √(4/h² − 3r_b²))/2. The total flat count π(r_b² + r_c²) is a
  function of state, falls as the centre grows, and is largest, π/h², for the empty space.

Standing after this stage: in an expanding space (a) no frame has the full unit (XU1-T1), (b) any count read
with the best frame's unit depends on the path, (c) which way it runs depends on whether the energy is read
by the same frame. (c) is the open choice; the framework's rule "one frame reads everything" picks F.

Put in: the best frame as reader; h as a variable. T3 is a sampled check of sign, not a proof over the region.
Not claimed: an arrow. Open gate: prove the sign of F over the whole region h·r_b < 1/√3, and state the
circuit it selects in plain terms.
