# DW1 — The defect, read on the centre potential, is the turn-part w

Sources read: UP5-T5 ([D_S,D_V] = αD_S+βD_V+F, w = ½[D_S,D_V]U), NC1, CP1, TT1 (sinh η = w),
BL1-T2 (one cycle adds exp(2η)), BL1 open gate (what selects the sense).

ε = S P_S /(V P_V). Two cross-corner pairs of scale readings.

- **DW1-T1.** [E[S|P], E[V|S]] = (V∂_V ε)·E[V|S], [E[V|P], E[S|V]] = (S∂_S ε⁻¹)·E[S|V].
  In UP5's form: α = 0, F = 0, β = the running of ε. The defect is pure frame part.
- **DW1-T2 (w from the potential).** w₁ = −½ (V∂_V ε)·PV, w₂ = ½ (S∂_S ε⁻¹)·TS.
  Turn-part = running of the response ratio × the work (heat) reading of the same axis.
- **DW1-T3 (one w at a self-similar centre).** a·ε²·PV·w₂ + b·TS·w₁ = 0: the two turn-parts are one.
- **DW1-T4 (steady turn).** At a self-similar centre w/U is constant along the scaling flow
  D = aS∂_S + bV∂_V. With TT1/BL1: every cycle on one flow line adds the same shared information.
- **DW1-T5.** With a scale (U = S²/(V−1) + S³) w/U is constant along no flow a:b — the turn runs.
- **DW1-T6 (sense).** Exchanging the order of the two readings sends w → −w; at a point w/U has a
  definite sign (witness: 1/4). The sense of the cycle is the sign of V∂_V ε, a property of the potential.

Reading of the BL1 gate: the sense is not chosen from outside; it is the direction in which the
response ratio runs.

Put in: the pure number used in TT1/BL1 is taken to be w/U (normalisation by the centre value).
Not claimed: a law of increase (BL1-T3 stands: the reverse order is algebraically allowed);
that w/U must keep one sign over the whole diagram.
Open gate: where V∂_V ε changes sign (the locus w = 0) and what the diagram does there.
