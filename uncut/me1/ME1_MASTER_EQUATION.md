# ME1 — One form for all planes, and its defect when the flatness conditions are dropped

Owner's direction for this stage: the potential at the centre of the diagram, generalised over every
flatness; relativity is one flatness condition, the other conditions are other symmetries; superimpose them
in one equation.

Sources read: PL1 (charge and spin planes), MT1 (momentum plane), XU2 (expansion plane), PL2 + XU3 (weight
part), ON1 + SS1 (frame part), UP5-T5, UP4 (F1–F4), SM1, UN2.

## 1. The flat case: one form, four planes (certified)

Centre with charge and spin, read by a moving reader: E(S, Q, J, p) = √(M(S,Q,J)² + p²).

- **ME1-T1.** T = T₀/γ, Φ = Φ₀/γ, Ω = Ω₀/γ, and the fourth partner is the velocity v = p/E.
- **ME1-T2.** The angles swept per closed turn, Φ·P and Ω·P, are the same for every reader.
- **ME1-T3.** dS = P·(dE − Φ·dQ − Ω·dJ − v·dp), with all six pair equations holding.

| plane | what is held symmetric | partner (angle per turn) | flatness condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| (E, t) | shift of the clock | — (the turn itself, 2π) | smooth centre F4 (SM1) |
| (p, x) | shift of place | v·P | pair equation with (E, t): MT1 |
| (J, angle) | turn of directions | Ω·P | pair equation: PL1 |
| (Q, gauge angle) | turn of the charge's phase | Φ·P | pair equation: PL1 |
| (h², volume) | stretch of scale | (r_b³/2)·P | pair equation: XU2 |
| all | one reader, unit 1 | k = 1 | UN2 |

## 2. The uncut case: one equation (certified as an identity)

Without assuming a potential: θ₀ = Σ aᵢ dqᵢ, and the reader's factor k = (angle/2π)/(unit). The count form is θ = k·θ₀ and

  **dθ = dk ∧ θ₀ + k·dθ₀** .

- first term: the weight part — one condition per plane (k constant along it);
- second term: the frame part — one pair equation per pair of planes.

**ME1-T5.** n planes carry n(n−1)/2 + n flatness conditions: 3, 6, 10, 15 for n = 2…5. Two planes give the three
of UP5-T5 (α, β, F). "Flat" is all of them at once; each can fail separately, and the stages so far have met:
frame part from a scale inside the potential (ON1, SS1), weight part from the reader's unit (XU1–XU3).

## 3. What this does and does not say about forces

Certified: the partner of each plane is an angle per closed turn, reader-free (T2). Reading (not certified):
a force is the partner's dependence on place — the gradient of an angle per turn. For (E, t) that is the
clock factor (MC1, MO1); for (Q, gauge angle) the potential Φ.
Not in this stage: planes whose turns do not commute among themselves (the three turns of directions already
do not; charges of that kind are the subject of the Yang-Mills line), where the frame part gains a term
quadratic in the partners. No claim is made about the weak or strong force.

## 4. Candidates for a prediction (none certified as new)

1. Cross terms: with four planes there are six pair equations; each off-diagonal one ties two partners
   (e.g. ∂Ω/∂Q = ∂Φ/∂J). Any system with two of the planes active gives a checkable equality.
2. Each failed condition has its own signature: frame part ⇒ a turn-part w and a defect proportional to the
   scale response (SS1); weight part ⇒ a count that depends on the path (XU3), bounded by the ledger (LG1).
3. A substance with exactly one built-in scale must satisfy SS1-T1 between measurable response ratios.

Put in: M(S, Q, J) for the centre with charge and spin (not derived in the line); MT1's boost.
Not claimed: a unified force law; any measured number.
