# BL1 — the balance of the shared information

Stage of the uncut line. No measurement. Exact (sympy): `bl1_balance_of_shared_information.py`, `test_bl1.py` (4 tests).

Continues CI1: when the shared information I rises, what is it exchanged for?

## Sources used (read, unchanged)

Publications `emk-ugd-algebra` EMK-1 (det = seen + lost); extra-ideas `uncut/ci1`, `tt1`; `physics/tl1` L2
(u_A N_A = u_B N_B), `ln1` N5.

## Statements

**T1 (the balance).** seen + lost = det in every frame, so exp(2 I_cut) − 1 = −lost/det. I rises exactly as
the lost channel grows. For a unit block seen = cosh²ψ and lost = −sinh²ψ: what the cut sees more is what it
has lost more, one for one.

**T2 (what one cycle adds).** For an open diagram with turn-part w = sinh η (TT1), starting from rest:

  exp(I₀) after k cycles = cosh(2kη);  after the first, 1 + 2w².

The step grows with k and tends to exp(ΔI) = exp(2η) = (ρ + t)/(ρ − t) per cycle. For w = 3/4:
17/8, 257/32, 4097/128, …, step factors 257/68, 4097/1028, … → 4. A self-dagger cut adds nothing.

**T3 (the reverse).** The reverse cycle returns to rest. The order of CI1-T5 is the order of repeating one
sense; the algebra does not forbid the other sense.

**T4 (between places).** TL1-L2 keeps u·N between places that exchange. With N = exp(−I): Log(u) − I is the
same at both places. Where I is larger the local unit of fluctuation is larger by exp(ΔI).

## What is put in, what is not claimed

* T3 is a refusal: no law of increase follows from the algebra alone. An arrow needs a statement that
  selects one sense; none is derived.
* T4 uses TL1's exchange law and the unit-block reading of the clock factor.
* No constant, no measured number.

## Open gate

What selects the sense of the cycle. The sign of w is the candidate: reversing the sense is w → −w, and a
system with a fixed w has one sense in which I rises.
