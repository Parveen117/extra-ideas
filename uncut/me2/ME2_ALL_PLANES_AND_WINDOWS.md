# ME2 — The remaining planes superimposed; the window law for every plane; two windows of one transition

Sources read: ME1 (four planes; master equation), XU2 (expansion plane), CR1 (window of the volume plane),
PL1, FD1, UP7 (the four mirrors of a cut), WD2 (three cuts).

## 1. Eight planes in one form (certified)

| reading | partner | plane first met in |
|---|---|---|
| count S | T (the turn itself) | ST1 |
| volume V | −P | UP-line (the diagram itself) |
| charge Q | Φ | PL1 |
| spin J | Ω | PL1 |
| momentum p | v | MT1 |
| magnetisation M | H | this stage |
| particle number N | μ | this stage (FD1-T2: the count unit is the ideal gas's scale) |

- **ME2-T1.** dS = P_t·(dE + P·dV − Φ·dQ − Ω·dJ − v·dp − H·dM − μ·dN − …): 28 pair equations, and with the 8
  weight conditions of ME1's master equation, 36 flatness conditions in all.

## 2. First-order transitions: one law for every plane (certified)

- **ME2-T2.** On a transition surface in (T, P, H): dT/dP = ΔV/ΔS, dT/dH = −ΔM/ΔS, and the cross relation
  ΔM/ΔV = −(∂T/∂H)/(∂T/∂P).
- **ME2-T3 (window law).** For a plane with field X and reading Y, inside the smeared window the turn-part is
  w_X → Y·T_N / (2·dT_N/dX), independent of the width, and the reading's own expansion coefficient changes sign
  exactly when the width is below δ*_X = |ΔY/Y| / (its background coefficient).
  CR1 is the case X = P.
- **ME2-T4 (two windows, one transition).** w_V / w_M = −(V/M)·(∂T/∂H)/(∂T/∂P): the windows of the volume
  plane and of the magnetic plane are tied by the two slopes of the same surface.

For chromium this gives a second test beside CR1: the field slope of T_N fixes the jump of magnetisation
through the pressure slope and the known ΔV; the stage has no measured field slope and makes no number of it.

## 3. What is still not superimposed

- Turns that do not commute among themselves (the three components of spin; charges of that kind): the frame
  part gains a term quadratic in the partners. Not done here.
- The mirrors (UP7's four, the reversal of sense): they are not planes with a continuous partner; how a
  mirror enters the count form is open.
- Planes whose partner depends on place (a force in ME1's reading): only the clock factor has been treated.

Put in: a potential E of the eight readings (flat case); uniform smearing of a step. Not claimed: any measured
value; a law for the non-commuting planes.
