# ON1 — Where non-commutation comes from: the running of the response ratio

Sources read: UP4/UP5 (w = ½[D_S,D_V]U), UP6 (eight scale operations E[x|y]; defects vanish for
power laws), UP1 (degree k), Morphic Algebra A-3 (cocycle ω, holonomy = local trace + pairwise defect).

## Statement
Work in logs: s = Log S, v = Log V, p = Log P(s,v). Scale readings
E[S|V] = ∂_s, E[V|S] = ∂_v, E[S|P] = ∂_s − ε ∂_v, E[V|P] = ∂_v − ε⁻¹ ∂_s, with the pure ratio
ε = p_s / p_v = −(∂Log V/∂Log S)_P.

- **ON1-T1 (law of the defect).** [E[S|V], E[S|P]] = −(∂_s ε)·E[V|S] and
  [E[V|S], E[V|P]] = −(∂_v ε⁻¹)·E[S|V]. Same-corner readings commute. So the only source of
  non-commutation is the *running* of the response ratio ε under scale.
- **ON1-T2 (when it is absent).** All defects vanish ⇔ ε is constant ⇔ P = F(S^c·V): the reading
  depends on one scaling combination only (self-similar). Pure power laws are a sub-case.
- **ON1-T3 (what switches it on).** P = S/(V−b): defect = b·V/(V−b)², exactly zero at b = 0.
  A built-in scale b at the centre of the diagram is what makes the cuts stop commuting.
- **ON1-T4 (flow form).** Reading the commutator on v returns −∂_s ε itself: the commutator
  coefficient *is* the flow function β = ∂ε/∂s of the ratio.

## Reading
Non-commutation is not an extra postulate: it is the potential at the centre failing to be
self-similar. λ = 0 / flat = constant ε. In A-3 language, ω is the accumulated ∂ε along a word.

## Put in / not claimed
Put in: two free directions (F3), smooth P (F4), p_s, p_v ≠ 0. Not claimed: why nature has a
scale b; identification of β with any field-theory β-function; any statement about ħ.
Open gate (answered in CP1: a self-similar centre keeps one defect, it does not commute): fixed points against exponents;
quantity that w (UP5) and the cycle growth (BL1) are functions of.
