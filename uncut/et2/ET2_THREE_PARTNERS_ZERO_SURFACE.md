# ET2 — Three partners: the line's rules against the published formula, the correction, and the surface T = 0

Sources read: ET1 (line T = 0; open gate), SP1 (spin as momentum of the boundary), LP1 (charge as stored cost),
XU1/XU2 (expansion: M = R(1 − h²R²)/2; unit 0 at 27h²M² = 1), CO1 (fall speed hX in an expanding space), UN2.

## (i) ET1's line is the usual condition

- **ET2-T1.** In the usual variables of a charged turning centre at its T = 0 end (a² + Q² = M²),
  Φ₀² + 2v² = 1 holds identically. ET1-T2 is a pass, not a new condition.

## (ii) Expansion as a third partner

Rest value by the stored-cost rule: M₀ = R/2 + Q²/(2R) − h²R³/2.

- **ET2-T2a.** Without spin, M₀² is exactly the published mass formula with a cosmological term: the stored-cost
  rule extends to expansion unchanged.
- **ET2-T2b (the momentum rule fails as it stands).** M₀² + (J/R)² is not the published formula.
- **ET2-T2c (the correction).** M₀² + (J/R)²·(1 − h²R²) is. The rim's momentum enters weighed by the
  expansion's clock factor at the rim, N² = 1 − (hR)².

So SP1's rule was the flat case of a rule with a clock factor in it. The factor was found by matching the
published formula, not derived; it is the same N² that CO1 gives a reader at distance R in an expanding space.

- **ET2-T3 (heat reading, three partners).** With Φ₀ = Q/R, x = hR, v² = (momentum term)/E²:
  T/T₀ = [(1 − v²)(1 − Φ₀² − 3x²) − v²(1 + Φ₀² − x²)/(1 − x²)] / √(1 − v²).
- **ET2-T4 (the surface T = 0).** (1 − v²)(1 − x²)(1 − Φ₀² − 3x²) = v²(1 + Φ₀² − x²).
  Its three ends: Φ₀ = 1 (charge), v = 1/√2 (turning), x = 1/√3 (expansion). The expansion end is XU1's state
  27h²M² = 1, where the best frame has unit 0.

The three ends say the same thing in three planes: the heat reading vanishes where a clock of the centre
reaches unit 0 — ET1 for turning, XU1 for expansion.

the extremal surface of that family, in the line's three pure numbers).
