# HD1 — Turning centres in any number of directions: SP1's rule refused beyond three, the form that holds, the law

Sources read: SP1, CF1 (rule 2; open gate: a case with more than one spin), ET1, ID1 (sharing 1/(d−1)), AL1 (degree
k = (d−2)/(d−1)), RT1 (rim reader's coin (cos χ, sin χ), unit N = cos χ), MT1.
External, read at the source: Emparan and Reall, "Black Holes in Higher Dimensions" (arXiv:0801.3471), eqs. (34),
(36)–(38), (45)–(47). Their d is ours plus one.

The published family, written in the rim reader's coin (rotation parameter a_i = r₊·tan χ_i):
(a) each turning plane has rim radius R_i = r₊/N_i; (b) M = T₀(r₊)·S/k; (c) J_i = (2/(d−1))·M·R_i·v_i.

- **HD1-T1 (heat-reading law).** 4π·r₊·T = (d−2) − 2·Σ v_i², certified for d = 3, 4, 5, 6 with 1, 2, 2, 3 planes,
  and equal to the source's eq. (38) for one plane in every d.
- **HD1-T2.** The partner of each spin is Ω_i = v_i/R_i (rim speed over rim radius), in every case.
- **HD1-T3 (the boost and its share).** At fixed count M = M₀·γ^{2/(d−1)} per turning plane. For d = 3 this is the
  plain boost of MT1/SP1; in general the exponent is 2/(d−1): a plane holds two of the d−1 directions of the boundary.
- **HD1-T4 (refusal).** In four directions M³, not M², is linear in J² at fixed count. SP1's energy-quadrature
  (and CF1's "rule 2" in that form) is special to three directions.
- **HD1-T5 (the surface T = 0).** Σ v_i² = (d−2)/2. One turning plane can reach it only for d = 3 (v² = ½, ET1);
  for d ≥ 4 a single plane has v < 1 ≤ (d−2)/2 and never does. Two planes in four directions: v₁² + v₂² = 1,
  which the source's extremal member satisfies.

Put in: (a), (b), (c) are read from the published family, not derived. Not claimed: the law as new physics — it is
the family's temperature in the line's variables; the stage's content is the form (−2v² per plane, ET1's term
repeated), the share 2/(d−1), and the refusal T4.

> Later note (TS2): inputs (b) and (c) follow from the first law once the rim rule is fixed (ρ = 1, or ID1's share 2/(d−1)); see `uncut/ts2`.
