# LC2 — The stored-cost rule in any number of directions

Sources read: LC1 (d = 3), MC1 (weight r^{d−2} between shells; r^−(d−2)), AL1, HD1.

- **LC2-T1.** A partner spread at least cost in d directions is q/r^{d−2} (d = 3…6).
- **LC2-T2.** The cost stored outside r is (d−2)q²/(2r^{d−2}).
- **LC2-T3.** With the centre value read at r equal to the far value less that cost, the horizon gives
  M = c·r₊^{d−2}(1 + Φ̂²) and 4π·r₊·T = (d−2)(1 − Φ̂²), Φ̂² the charge term at the horizon. Rule 1 holds in every d
  without change.
- **LC2-T4.** Three directions, charge and turning together, at the same horizon radius:
  4π·r₊·T = 1 − 2v² − Φ₀². The two planes add.

Put in: LC1's joining rule; count normalised by the neutral T₀ = (d−2)/(4πr₊). The forms agree with the known
static charged centres in d directions.
