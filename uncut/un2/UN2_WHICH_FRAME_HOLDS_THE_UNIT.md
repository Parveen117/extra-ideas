# UN2 — Which frame holds the unit: correction of UN1-T4

Sources read: GR1 (share p = (1+N)/2; N² + m = 1; horizon = balanced share), PR4 (j = (2p−1)n,
σ² = n²·4p(1−p)), CL1/HB1 (coin (c, s); unit = cone speed c), UN1, HX1-T3, AL1.

- **UN2-T1 (the legs of the coin at a place).** j/n = N and σ/n = √(1−N²) = fall speed.
  In the line's own dictionary the speed-like leg of the coin is the clock factor N, not the fall speed.
- **UN2-T2.** Far frame: N = 1, coin (1, 0): the full unit 1 (HB1). Static frame at the horizon: N = 0,
  the quarter-turn clock: unit 0, no minimum there.
- **UN2-T3 (what UN1-T4 did).** It took the other leg (fall speed → 1). The two legs are one coin a quarter
  turn apart — the same quarter turn by which the horizon exchanges the cuts (HX1-T3). UN1's assumption (b)
  is withdrawn: it contradicts T1.
- **UN2-T4 (the quarter, with the unit where it belongs).** M and T of AL1 are far readings (κ is the rate
  against the far clock). The far frame's unit is 1 by T2. Count = area/4 follows with no assumption about
  the frame at the horizon.
- **UN2-T5.** Reading the same T with the unit N of a static frame at finite r multiplies the result by
  1/N = n, GR1's blueshift.

Chain inputs after this stage: (a) the reading T is one unit per closed turn; (c) directions = cuts.
UN1's (b) is no longer used.
Not claimed: T5 as a law for local temperature — it is the ratio of two unit choices; a local statement
needs the energy read by the same frame.
Open gate: (a). In BL1 a closed turn adds no shared information and an open cycle adds exp(2η); express
T as the exchange rate between the two — M per unit of I — and compare with u·κ.

> Later note (checklist, 8 Oct): T2 and T5 treat the clock factor as the unit of the count; that conflicts with DC1-K1 and TL1-L2 and is withdrawn. T1 (legs of the coin) and the quarter obtained with one unit stand. See `uncut/CHECKLIST_AGAINST_THE_LINE.md`.
