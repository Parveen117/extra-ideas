# AR1 — The accelerated reader: its temperature, and a count that does not depend on the reader

Sources read: PR3 (uniform acceleration is N = ρ), QT1-T1 (N = κρ near a horizon), HX1 + SM1 (boost and
closing turn are one Exp; the period 2π/κ is forced), ST1-T4 (T = 1/period; count = (energy, time)-area per
unit turn), QT2-T4 (a closed reading exchanges nothing), SS1 (no scale ⇒ no defect), CI1.

A family of readers at heights ρ; reference reader at ρ₀; N = ρ/ρ₀.

- **AR1-T2.** The closed turn has period 2πρ in the time of the reader at ρ — for every reader.
- **AR1-T3.** T(ρ) = 1/(2πρ) = (the reader's own acceleration)/(one turn). T·N is the same for all readers.
- **AR1-T4 (the count is reader-free).** Energy read at ρ scales as 1/ρ, the period as ρ: the
  (energy, time)-area of the closed turn is the same for every reader. T and energy depend on who reads;
  their area does not.
- **AR1-T5.** The family has no scale: every pure number is unchanged when ρ and ρ₀ are stretched together.
  By SS1 it carries no defect.
- **AR1-T6.** Open reading of the same family: I = Log cosh(t/ρ₀) grows; the closed reading adds none.

Answers, in the line's terms, to "is this an equilibrium" and "is the entropy well defined":
the closed reading is the one that exchanges nothing (QT2-T4), and the count is the area in the
(energy, time) plane, which no change of reader inside the family alters.

Put in: T = 1/period and count = plane area (ST1); the family N = ρ/ρ₀ (PR3). Not claimed: a count per unit
of boundary for this family (that needs the relation between energy crossing and boundary growth, which the
line has only for a centre, AL1); the constant in physical units.
