# PL1 — The count as area in two planes: (M, t) and (charge or spin, its angle)

Sources read: ST1 (count = (M, t)-area; open gate: the extremal count), BH1, BH2, UP5-T1 (a layer has its
Maxwell pattern exactly when its two axes carry the same area form), QT1.

P = period of the closed turn. Over one period the charge direction sweeps the angle Φ·P, the rotation
direction the angle Ω·P.

- **PL1-T1 / T3 (one equation).** ∂P/∂Q = −∂(ΦP)/∂M and ∂P/∂J = −∂(ΩP)/∂M: the two planes carry one area
  form. This single equation of the Maxwell type is what makes the count independent of the path.
- **PL1-T2 (charged).** count = [(M, t)-area] − [(Q, angle)-area] = 4πM² − ∫ΦP dQ, exactly πr₊².
  At the extremal end the charge plane holds 3πM², leaving πM².
- **PL1-T4 (rotating).** count = 4πM² − ∫ΩP dJ = 2πM·r₊. At the extremal end the spin plane holds 2πM²,
  leaving 2πM².
- **PL1-T5.** At the extremal end Φ → 1 and Ω → 1/(2M).

ST1's gate is closed: the extremal count needs no convention for its zero. It is what remains of the
(M, t)-area after the second plane has taken its share — three quarters for charge, one half for spin.
With BH1/BH2: the charged extremal horizon is at rest and keeps ¼ of the neutral count; the rotating one
still turns and keeps ½.

Put in: the centres M(S,Q), M(S,J) (BH1, BH2); count = area per unit turn (ST1). Not claimed: why the shares
are ¾ and ½ beyond the computation.
