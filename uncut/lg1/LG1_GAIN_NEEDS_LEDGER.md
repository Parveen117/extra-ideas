# LG1 — A count gain needs an enclosed area, and an enclosed area costs history ledger

Sources read: GE2-T3 (Publications, generalized-euler-evolution: symmetric steps ±v; ledger
h = ½Σw|v|², additive, "a path and its reversed point motion can close at the endpoint while retaining a
positive history ledger"), XU3 (dθ = w(x)·dS ∧ dx; circuit law).

- **LG1-T1.** Out and back along one direction: the count form returns exactly to its start; the ledger of
  the two turns is positive. History passes with no count gained.
- **LG1-T2.** The smallest history that gains count has two directions and four legs (+v, +w, −v, −w).
  Example S: 3 → 4, x: 1/10 → 3/20: gain 0.16643467, ledger 0.250625.
- **LG1-T3 (bound).** gain ≤ ΔS·Δx·w_max ≤ 2·w_max·ledger. No count is gained without ledger; the reverse
  is false (T1).
- **LG1-T4.** Exchanging the order of the two directions reverses the gain and leaves the ledger unchanged.

Reading: the framework has two one-way quantities — the history ledger (always grows, GE2) and the count
(grows in one sense of circuit, XU3). The second is bounded by the first and needs two non-commuting
directions; the first needs nothing.

Put in: step sizes measured in the chart (S, x) with GE2's weight convention — the bound's constant depends
on that chart; the inequality's form does not. Not claimed: an equality between ledger and count.
