# XU3 — The sense is proved over the whole region; the gain of a circuit factorises; the slow-expansion law

Sources read: XU1, XU2 (reading F: one frame reads unit, time and energy; sign sampled, not proved), PL2
(dθ = dk ∧ dS), BL1 (open gate: what selects the sense), UN2.

Two variables carry everything: the flat count S = πr_b² and the pure number x = h·r_b (0 < x < 1/√3).
Then h·M = u(x) = x(1 − x²)/2 and the unit of the best frame is c(x) = √(1 − 3u^{2/3}).

- **XU3-T1.** P·M = S·g(x), g = 2(1 − x²)/(1 − 3x²): the frame-consistent form is θ = dS/c − S·g·dc/c².
- **XU3-T2 (closed form of the defect).** dθ = (1 + x²) / (2·u^{1/3}·c³) · dS ∧ dx. (Symbolic identity.)
- **XU3-T3 (proof of the sense).** Every factor is positive on 0 < x < 1/√3. The defect has one sign on the
  whole region; XU2's sampled statement is now a theorem. Reading A of XU1 has the opposite sign throughout.
- **XU3-T4 (circuit law).** The count gained in one circuit is a product:
  gain = (S₂ − S₁)·(Φ(x₂) − Φ(x₁)),  Φ′(x) = (1 + x²)/(2u^{1/3}c³).
  Example (S: π → 4π, x: 1/10 → 1/5): 3.6063057.
  In words: the count rises when the centre grows while x is small and shrinks while x is large —
  grow when the space is expanding slowly for your size, give back when it is expanding fast.
- **XU3-T5 (slow expansion).** Φ(x) → (3/2)·(x/2)^{2/3} = (3/2)·(h·M)^{2/3}. The exponent 2/3 is exact.
- **XU3-T6.** No expansion, no defect: Φ(0) = 0. In a space with a frame of full unit the count is a
  function of state (UN2); the path-dependence is entirely the expansion's.

What stands: BL1 asked what selects the sense of the cycle and could not get it from the algebra. Here it
is selected, with a proof, by three things already in the line: the unit of a frame is its clock factor
(UN2), one frame reads everything, and an expanding space has no frame with the full unit (XU1).

Put in: the reader is the best frame; h is a variable of state; the centres and counts of AL1–PL1.
Not claimed: that a physical process runs such a circuit; that this is the observed arrow of time; any
measured number. BL1's multiplicative growth and this additive gain are not yet one statement.
