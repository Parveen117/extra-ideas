# TS2 — The share of the centre value carried by a turning plane is fixed by the first law

Sources read: HD1 (three structural inputs read from the published family; open gate: derive them), ID1 (one
transverse direction carries 1/(d−1) of the radial), SP1, MT1, HX1 (the turned cut cos χ·C₁ + sin χ·C₃), UN2/RT1
(rim reader's unit N = cos χ), CP1 (one scale: weights), AL1 (count = boundary measure; degree k).

Set-up, with three exponents left free:
- rim radius R = r·γ^ρ (γ = 1/cos χ); count S = s·r^{d−3}·R² (boundary measure: the plane's two directions at R,
  the other d−3 at r);
- M = M₀(S)·γ^σ at fixed count; J = κ·M·R·v; partner Ω = v/R; one scale.

- **TS2-T1.** The first law holds for every χ exactly when σ = κ = 1 − ρ·(d−3)/(d−1) (d = 3…6).
  The exponent of the boost and the share of M that turns are the same number, and it is fixed by ρ.
- **TS2-T2.** In three directions it is 1 for every ρ: the plain boost of MT1/SP1 needs no rule for the rim.
  This is why SP1 worked, and why three directions cannot tell the rules apart.
- **TS2-T3.** ρ = 1 — the inner radius is the rim radius read with the rim reader's unit, r = N·R — gives the share
  2/(d−1), M = (d−1)S/(4πr), J = (2/(d−1))·M·R·v and 4π·r·T = (d−2) − 2v²: HD1's three inputs and its law.
- **TS2-T4.** ρ = 0 (no contraction) is also consistent: plain boost in every d, with another heat reading
  (d = 5: 4cos χ − 1/cos χ). The first law alone does not choose.
- **TS2-T5.** Two planes, ρ = 1, d = 4 and 5: the same share for each plane.
- **TS2-T6.** ρ = 1 is HX1's turned cut: inner radius and turning length are the two components of the rim length.
- **TS2-T7.** Conversely ID1's equal sharing — a plane holds two of the d−1 transverse directions, share 2/(d−1) —
  fixes ρ = 1 for d ≠ 3.

Standing of HD1's inputs: (b) and (c) are no longer read from the published family; they follow from the
forms above and one statement, which can be taken either as ρ = 1 (the turned cut) or as ID1's share.

Put in: the forms (count as boundary measure with one rim radius per plane; boost as a power of γ; J ∝ M·R·v;
Ω = v/R; one scale). Without the power form the first law leaves the share free (a one-parameter family).
Not claimed: a proof that nature's centre has ρ = 1 from inside the line beyond T6/T7's two readings.
