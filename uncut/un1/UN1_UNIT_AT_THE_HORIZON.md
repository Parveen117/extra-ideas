# UN1 — The unit of the horizon frame, and the chain for the count

Sources read: HB1 (minimum = ½·even part of the tick; unit = cone speed = 1 − curvature/2; ladder of clocks),
MO1 (fall speed 1 at the horizon), AL1, QT1, QT3, HX1, WD1, WD2.

- **UN1-T1.** HB1's ladder: unit of a frame with a Q-mark clock = cos(2π/Q): −1, 0, 1/√2, … → 1.
- **UN1-T2 (refusal).** One tick is the turn 2π/Q, never a full turn for a real clock. The test proposed at
  the end of HX1 fails as stated.
- **UN1-T3 (the count with the frame's own unit).** HB1's minimum ½·c_f per closed turn gives
  count = area/(4·c_f).
- **UN1-T4 (at the horizon).** The frame there is light-like (fall speed 1), and HB1 gives a light-like frame
  the full unit 1. So count = area/4, with no choice of clock.
- **UN1-T5.** An eight-mark clock would give area·√2/4; the quarter-turn clock (unit 0) gives no finite count.
- **UN1-T6 (uncut form).** With centre angle α and unit c_f: count = (area/4)·(α/2π)/c_f.
