# HL1 — The heat-reading law: one form for every plane

Sources read: HD1, LC2, ET1, ET2, QT1 (κ = ½∂_r N²; T = κ per turn), MC1 (the memory's power r^−(d−2)),
NC1/SC1 (a defect is the running of a pure number under scale), UP6 (scale reading r∂_r).

Clock factor N² = P(r) − m·r^−(d−2). P holds all the planes: stored costs enter as a sum of terms c_j·r^{p_j},
turnings as a product of factors (1 + a_i²/r²). N_rim² = Π cos²χ_i is the product of the rim readers' units.

- **HL1-T1 (static planes).** 4π·r₊·T = (d−2) + Σ_j (p_j + d − 2)·ε_j, ε_j the value of plane j's term at the horizon.
  The weight p_j + d − 2 is the plane's degree measured from the memory's own (T4).
- **HL1-T2.** Charge: weight −(d−2); expansion: weight d on −x². In three directions 1 − Φ̂² − 3x² (ET2 without turning).
- **HL1-T3.** A plane with the memory's own power contributes nothing.
- **HL1-T5.** T = 0 ends: Φ̂² = 1; x² = (d−2)/d.
- **HL1-T6/T7 (turning planes).** The same expression with P = Π(1 + a_i²/r²), times N_rim², gives
  (d−2) − 2Σv_i² for one plane in every d and for two and three planes.
- **HL1-T8.** Charge and turning in three directions: cos 2χ − Φ₀².
- **HL1-T9 (one form).**

    4π·r₊·T = N_rim² · [ (d−2)·P + r·∂_r P ] at r₊ .

In the line's words: the heat reading of a centre is the neutral value (d−2) times the planes' factor, plus the
running of that factor under scale, read with the rim readers' units. A centre departs from the neutral heat
reading exactly as far as its planes' factor runs — the same notion of defect as NC1/SC1 — and T = 0 is where
r^{d−2}·P stops running.

Put in: the clock factor in the form P − m·r^−(d−2); T = κ per turn (QT1/SM1). Certified cases are those listed;
a centre with charge and turning together in four or more directions is not among them (no closed form is
used or proposed). Not claimed: the individual formulas as new — they are the known heat readings; the
stage's content is the single form and its reading as a running.
