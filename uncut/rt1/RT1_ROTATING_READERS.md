# RT1 — Readers on a rotating system

Sources read: UN2 (unit of a reader = its clock factor; coin N² + m = 1), GR1, PL1 (spin plane: dS = P·dE − ΩP·dJ),
ST1-T4, AR1, HX1 (tanh ψ = speed), MT1.

Co-rotating reader at radius r: rim speed ωr, clock N = √(1 − ω²r²) against the reader on the axis.

- **RT1-T1.** N² + (ωr)² = 1: the same coin as the static centre, with m = (ωr)².
- **RT1-T2.** A closed turn shared by the system has period N·P₀ for the reader at r: T(r)·N(r) is constant.
- **RT1-T3 (the rim).** The reader's unit is N: 1 on the axis, 0 at ωr = 1. There the clock is the quarter-turn
  clock of HB1 — no minimum, no count can be read, and 1/T = 0. The surface ωr = 1 is to a rotating system
  what the horizon is to the static reader (UN2-T2): the end of the readers, not a place with a temperature.
- **RT1-T4.** With the spin plane the count is reader-free: N·P₀ × (dE − ω·dJ)/N = P₀(dE − ω·dJ).
- **RT1-T5 (equilibrium is rigid).** Two parts exchanging energy and spin at fixed totals: the count is
  stationary for every exchange exactly when they share the period and the rate ω.
- **RT1-T6.** In HX1's terms the reader at r is the axis cut turned by the imaginary angle ψ, tanh ψ = ωr;
  the angle is unbounded at the rim.

Put in: co-rotating readers with rim speed ωr; T = 1/period; PL1's spin plane. Not claimed: what a system
larger than 1/ω does (the readers end; nothing here continues them).
