# QT2 — When the four-step walk closes as a turn

Sources read: UP7 (walk element ικ of a cut κ = uK+vS+wι), TT1/BL1 (growth per cycle), QT1 (input (i):
the horizon cycle read by the turn), MO1/GB1 (unit block, tanh ψ = fall speed), TS1/HM1-T5 (past the horizon).

- **QT2-T1 (walk law, any cut).** W = ικ obeys W² + 2wW − (u²+v²−w²) = 0; its two values are −w ± √(u²+v²).
- **QT2-T2 (refusal).** For a cut with real parts u²+v² ≥ 0: the walk is a boost or null, never a turn.
  QT1's input (i) cannot be obtained from a real cut. DW1's real w does not put the horizon on the closing side.
- **QT2-T3 (what closing needs).** The walk is a pure turn exactly when the seen part of the cut sits in the
  lost channel: u²+v² = −s², w² + s² = 1; values −w ± ιs of modulus 1.
- **QT2-T4.** Such a walk adds no shared information (growth factor 1): closed ⇔ nothing exchanged.
- **QT2-T5 (the frame cut across the horizon).** κ = cosh ψ·K + sinh ψ·ι, tanh ψ = fall speed.
  Outside: values e^{−ψ}, −e^{ψ} — a boost. Past the horizon (fall speed > 1, ψ → ψ′ + ιπ/2 in the
  cut-complex carrier): values −ι·e^{∓ψ′} — a quarter turn times a boost. The turn appears exactly on
  crossing; four walks make the full turn. As the fall speed → ∞ (ψ′ → 0) the walk is the pure quarter turn.

Result: closing is not a property the outside can have; it is what the walk becomes when seen and lost
exchange. Open reading (growth, an order) and closed reading (a period, a temperature) are the two sides
of one cut parameter.
