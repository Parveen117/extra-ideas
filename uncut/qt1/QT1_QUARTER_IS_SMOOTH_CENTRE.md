# QT1 — The quarter of the horizon count is the smooth-centre condition

Sources read: AL1 (S = area/(8π·u)), TT1 (cycle = boost Exp(−2ηn)), UP7 (cuts κ = uK+vS+wι), RMG10
(response space, signature (2,1)), RMG11 (two-valued transport), UP4 (flatness list F1–F4), CI1 (cycle count as clock).

- **QT1-T1.** Near the horizon the clock rate is N = κ·ρ (ρ = proper distance), in every dimension.
- **QT1-T2.** At fixed ρ the frame's orbit in the reading time is a boost Exp(κt·S), S² = +1: open, it never returns.
- **QT1-T3.** Read by the turn (ι² = −1) the same orbit closes, first at angle 2π; at π it is −1.
- **QT1-T4 (F4 at the horizon).** A cycle of period P sweeps the angle κP; its circle at radius ρ has
  length κPρ. No cone at the centre (length/radius = 2π) ⇔ P = 2π/κ.
- **QT1-T5 (the quarter).** One unit per closed cycle, T = 1/P, gives u = 1/(κP) and
  S = (area/4)·(κP/2π). With F4: S = area/4 exactly. Without F4 the count is the quarter times
  (angle at the centre / 2π).
- **QT1-T6.** (uK+vS+wι)² = u²+v²−w²: a generator closes only when its turn-part dominates.

What this settles: the ¼ is not a separate constant. It is flatness assumption F4 (smooth at the centre)
applied at the horizon, with the cycle count as clock. The uncut statement is T5's general form.
