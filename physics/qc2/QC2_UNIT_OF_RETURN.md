# QC2 — The unit of return: the fluctuation scale turns the pair into operators

QC1 ended with integers but no scale. This stage gives the pair
(w, p) = (extensive offsets, intensive offsets) the one scale

that scale does.

## 1. Statements

Ensemble: probe offsets distributed as exp(−wᵀHw / 2κ).

**T1 (the intensive offset becomes a derivative).** For every polynomial
reading f,

```text
⟨ p_i f(w) ⟩ = κ ⟨ ∂f/∂w_i ⟩ ,        hence   p ≙ κ ∂/∂w ,   [κ∂_i , w_j] = κ δ_ij ,   ⟨p·w⟩ = 2κ .
```

Inside the ensemble the classical canonical pair of QC1 is an operator
pair whose commutator is the scale κ.

**T2 (number and content).** In the H-orthonormal probe plane,
z = y₁ + iy₂, the Wick-ordered readings

```text
:z^a z̄^b: = Σ_k (−2κ)^k k! C(a,k) C(b,k) z^{a−k} z̄^{b−k}
```

are orthogonal with norms a! b! (2κ)^{a+b}; each has a definite number
N = a + b under z∂_z + z̄∂_z̄ − 4κ∂_z∂_z̄ (minus the relaxation generator
κΔ − y·∇) and a definite content q = a − b under z∂_z − z̄∂_z̄. Number N
carries the contents q = −N, −N+2, …, N.

**T3 (the return generator).** y₁p₂ − y₂p₁ with p = κ∂ is κ times the
content operator in the circular direction: its spectrum is κ·q, and the
return of a cycle acts on the reading (N, q) by Exp(qΘ) in the circular
sector. The return of QC1 is generated by an operator whose quantum is κ.

*Proof.* T1 is integration by parts in the ensemble. T2: the Wick
polynomials are the orthogonal polynomials of the isotropic ensemble of
variance κ; both operators act diagonally on z^{a−k}z̄^{b−k} and the
κ-terms telescope. T3: y₁∂₂ − y₂∂₁ = i(z∂_z − z̄∂_z̄), and rotations
commute with Wick ordering, so QC1-T3 applies to :z^a z̄^b:. ∎

So with a scale present, QC1's integers are counts: N quanta of size 2κ
in wᵀHw, and q units of κ in the return generator. On the thermal side

```text
κ = k_B T   (energy representation),        κ = k_B   (entropy representation).
```

The commutator is real here. The same pair with the unit placed on the
circular generator (κ·R, R² = −1) is the quantum form; this stage does not
derive that placement.

## 2. Which representation carries a constant unit

In the energy representation the unit k_BT changes along a cycle. In the
entropy representation, offsets (δu, δv) with form −D²s, the unit is the
constant k_B. The two representations have different returns:

```text
fluid   cycle (PH1 Table 1)     Θ energy     Θ entropy     Θ entropy, ideal gas with c_v⁰(T)
CO₂     310–350 K, 200–700      −0.9260      −1.0045        0   (7·10⁻⁸)
Ar      155–200 K, 250–800      −1.0728      −1.2444        0   (2·10⁻⁸)
```

- Both routes (loop formula, direct transport) agree to 10⁻⁶ in each
  representation.
- In the entropy representation the ideal gas is flat for **any** c_v(T).
  The small vibrational return of PH1 (−0.007 for CO₂) is absent: with the
  constant unit, the whole return is interaction.
