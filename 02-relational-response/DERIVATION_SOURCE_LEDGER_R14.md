## Citation rule

Use `theorem ID — origin; required native data or imported structure; evidence scope; commit/path`. A theorem called “native” is not accepted solely because of its title. A coordinate formula with a classical name is not rejected solely because of that name.

`NATIVE_DERIVED` means the object and proof use declared cut/refinement/path structures before the classical counterpart is introduced. Ordinary logic, finite counting, induction and specified completion arguments remain metatheory. It does not mean derivation without premises, physical uniqueness, or external independent certification.

`NATIVE_CONSTRUCTION` marks a deliberately defined object or protocol whose properties are proved. It prevents a definition of content or a choice of contrast from being misrepresented as its unique physical selection. `CLASSICAL_IMPORT` and `MIXED_ADAPTER` name the actual external structures in the citation itself. `COORDINATE_SHADOW` requires a proved bridge, not a similarity of notation.
