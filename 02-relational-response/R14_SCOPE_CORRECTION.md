R14 §10's varying-source examples distinguish members of its restricted family. They do not prove that the full UGD/recognition framework lacks a source-selection law or that every possible native construction is underdetermined. That extrapolation is withdrawn. The scoped examples remain useful checks within their stated scalar/preparation/protocol inputs.
