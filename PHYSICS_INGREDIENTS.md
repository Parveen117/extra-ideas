[NT-1–NT-8][nt] defines connection curvature by the transport-order defect

\[
F_{ij}=[\nabla_i,\nabla_j]-\nabla_{[\delta_i,\delta_j]},
\quad
F_{ij}=\delta_iA_j-\delta_jA_i+[A_i,A_j]-c_{ij}^{k}A_k.
\]

For the specified positive response element H and strain connection
A^H=H⁻¹dH/2, it derives F^H=-[H⁻¹∂_iH,H⁻¹∂_jH]/4. Only after the faithful
tangent/Hessian adapter, H=D²u and torsion-free metric compatibility does that
particular represented connection become Levi–Civita. General native curvature
is not defined by classical Riemann curvature. NT also retains adjoint Smriti
balance and the observer's complementary-return term.

can be flat while its represented de Sitter/anti-de Sitter metric is curved.

flatness is classical flatness' or 'observation always removes curvature'.

## Yang–Mills ledger precision

YM-41/NG-1 names the t-direction as the remaining operator wall **inside the
declared column/content contract**. RKF T75 explicitly retains a declared
Gram-uniform tail component above content level 2. Thus 'content delivered'
does not mean every untruncated analytic estimate is unconditional. The true
chain upper bound on all superpositions, asymptotically-free cutoff trajectory
and native-to-Clay dictionary remain distinct obligations.

The official Clay problem requires a nontrivial four-dimensional quantum
Yang–Mills theory with the required axiomatic properties and a strictly
positive mass gap for every compact simple gauge group. See
[Jaffe–Witten](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf).
Our U(n)/SU(2) carrier bridge, finite gaps and conditional free-field gap do not
by themselves establish that statement. Quantum gravity likewise requires an
actual quantum theory of dynamical geometry, not only an Einstein equation or
a formal effective-action derivative.
