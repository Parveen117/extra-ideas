\[

\quad
F_{ij}=\delta_iA_j-\delta_jA_i+[A_i,A_j]-c_{ij}^{k}A_k.
\]

For the specified positive response element H and strain connection
A^H=H⁻¹dH/2, it derives F^H=-[H⁻¹∂_iH,H⁻¹∂_jH]/4. Only after the faithful
tangent/Hessian adapter, H=D²u and torsion-free metric compatibility does that
particular represented connection become Levi–Civita. General native curvature
is not defined by classical Riemann curvature. NT also retains adjoint Smriti
balance and the observer's complementary-return term.

flatness is classical flatness' or 'observation always removes curvature'.
